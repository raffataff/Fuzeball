The triggers are one axis — LT finesse ◀ ▶ RT power — and holding both cancels back to a normal swing. A charge has a sweet band: the marker over your rod swells gold and the charge tick brightens when you are in it. Hold past it and the rod trembles, the shot loses power and it sprays.
In Total Control the same two triggers ALSO size the slide step — LT fine, RT fast — and the right stick becomes the swing: pull back and flick forward. Hold both triggers with the stick pulled back to charge that swing, and the free right-stick axis bends the shot.

Graphics

Resolution renders the game at a fraction of your screen and upscales it — the biggest speed-up on laptops and integrated graphics. Shadows and reflections are the next heaviest effects. Lower any of these if the frame rate dips.

Effects

Ball trails and impact / goal particles are pure spectacle — turn them off to shave a little more off busy moments. Fog is the haze each room fades into the distance with; switch it off to see a room's far wall and the props out at its edges. Reduced effects strips the expensive glow, blur and animated shadows from the on-screen notifications and menus while keeping the motion.
