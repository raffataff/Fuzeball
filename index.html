Graphics

Resolution renders the game at a fraction of your screen and upscales it — the biggest speed-up on laptops and integrated graphics. Shadows and reflections are the next heaviest effects. Lower any of these if the frame rate dips.

Effects

Ball trails and impact / goal particles are pure spectacle — turn them off to shave a little more off busy moments. Reduced effects strips the expensive glow, blur and animated shadows from the on-screen notifications and menus while keeping the motion.
