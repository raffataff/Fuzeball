# Fuzeball Carapace

A faceted petrol-green aluminium shell sits around a straight playing cassette. The shell narrows at midfield and flares toward the goals. Two splayed, welded steel portal frames give the table its stance. Copper-coloured strips mark the breaks in the shell.

## Geometry

The [game configuration](../../js/config.js#L162) supplies the pitch and goal dimensions. This proposal assumes 10 mm per game unit and interprets the game’s nominal mouth dimensions as the physical clear opening.

| --- | --- |

| Goal pocket depth | 90 mm per end |

| Maximum shell width | About 795 mm, below the controls |
| Pitch height | 850 mm above floor |

## Construction

1. Build the inner cassette from 24 mm birch ply rails and an 18 mm laminate-faced deck on crossmembers. Drill opposite bearing seats together from a common jig. Keep straight rail faces through the eight rod axes.
2. Cut and press-brake three 2 mm aluminium panels per long side. Use straight bends, folded return edges and M6 fasteners into captive inserts on separate mounting brackets. Keep the top of the shell below pitch height and verify hand clearance through the entire rod stroke. The panels carry their own weight.
3. Weld each splayed portal from mitred rectangular steel tube. Start with 50 × 30 mm tube for the prototype and a stance around 820 mm across the levelling shoes. Bolt the portals to structural crossmembers beneath the cassette and link them with a longitudinal tube to resist racking. Validate tube and joint sizing on the prototype.
4. Fit the two nets from the pitch side using screw-fixed retainers. Add removable corner ramps outside the keeper’s sweep and roll-test them with the 38 mm ball.
5. Retain the 1–2–3–5–5–3–2–1 formation. Start with per-side rod strokes of 110 mm for keepers, 180 mm for defenders, 115 mm for forwards and 62 mm for midfielders. Confirm the available bearing, bumper and handle clearances before cutting the shafts.
