# Fuzeball: live demo outline (keyboard and mouse)

About 4 minutes. Start the game before the session so nothing loads on stage.

Before you start:
- Audio on, and check the volume in the room first.
- Have the trailer ready as the fallback if the game stalls.
- Default room is the Starport Arcade.

## Controls to say out loud

- Slide the rod: arrows or W/S, or move the mouse.
- Switch rod: Q/E, or the mouse wheel.
- Kick: Space or left mouse button.
- Raise: Left Shift or right mouse button.
- Power: Right Shift (hold to wind up a shot).
- Finesse: Right Ctrl (hold for pins and receives).

## Beats

1. **Kick Off, 30 seconds.** Start a match against the AI. Slide, switch rods, and score one goal to show the basics.
2. **Power shot, 20 seconds.** Wind up a shot from the attacking rod and let it go.
3. **Back pin and tic-tac, 60 seconds.** Catch a ball from behind with finesse and raise. Pass it on or bounce it between two men.
4. **Forward pin, 30 seconds.** Hold finesse and kick to pose the forward pin, then let go.
5. **Replay, 20 seconds.** Show the replay of the goal or the save.
6. **Training drill, 40 seconds.** Open Training and run one drill, such as Tic-tac Lane or Forward Pin.
7. **League screen, 20 seconds.** Show the League lobby: Season, Squad and Club tabs. Don't go into a season.

## If something goes wrong

- If a match freezes, switch to the trailer.
- If the AI does something odd, say it's the AI and move on.
- Keep a sentence ready for the room if a goal happens during the demo.
