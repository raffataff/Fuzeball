# Fuzeball: War Room talking points

Target: about 10 minutes. Sections are sized so the demo gets the most time.

---

## 1. What it is (1 min)

- Fuzeball is a 3D foosball game for Steam Early Access.
- The table is wood, worn and chipped. It came from Earth.
- The venues are small, loud and a bit grubby. The ceremony around them is enormous: gold trophies, anthems, a rulebook thicker than the table.
- Table football, taken far too seriously.

## 2. Trailer (under 1 min)

- Play the 35-second trailer.
- Say nothing over it.

## 3. Live demo (about 4 min)

See `demo-outline.md`.

## 4. Where it stands (1 min)

- I've built it and tested it heavily myself.
- Nobody outside has played it yet.
- No Steam page, no Next Fest submission, no release date yet.

## 5. Getting players (1 min)

- Socials, starting with the trailer.
- An itch.io version.
- A website hub that links to both.

## 6. What's next (1 min)

- League: sponsors, more upgrade mechanics, and whole-rod swaps for stamina and injuries.
- Injuries come from wear during a match, or random events between matches.
- A standalone tournament mode, probably the Champions Cup moved out of League.
- Sound: the SFX Lab for UI and effects, and the Sound Lab for the table sounds.

## 7. Questions for the room (remaining time)

- The Champions Cup might become the Federation Cup. Keep the name or change it?
- Injuries: should wear from a match and random events between matches both count, or pick one?
- What would make you want to play a match of this, and what would make you stop?
